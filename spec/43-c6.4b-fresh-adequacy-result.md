## Parent decision

Both hardware profiles are inadequate, so C6.4b is:

```text
INADEQUATE_REVISE_REPRESENTATION
```

This conclusion does not depend solely on the two out-of-domain transfer axes. Every transfer evaluated subset already violates the frozen error oracle, A100 prefill violates max APE, and H100 prefill violates both MAPE and max APE.

No clipping, smoothing, boundary narrowing, knot addition, refit, axis movement, threshold change, or interpolation change was applied after observing the fresh result.

## Consequence

C6.4c ordinary simulator integration remains blocked because no complete A100/H100 representation-v2 profile satisfies the declared adequacy contract.

The C6.4a fresh boundary has now been observed and becomes diagnostic history. A subsequent representation revision must not tune against these fresh values and then reuse them as an unbiased validation boundary. Any replacement representation must be justified independently of these residuals and must establish a new untouched evaluation boundary before its next adequacy test.
