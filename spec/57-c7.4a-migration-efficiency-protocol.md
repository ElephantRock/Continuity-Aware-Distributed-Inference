The classification is strictly `TRANSFER_FASTER`, `RECOMPUTE_FASTER`, or `TIE`. Track A carries no policy ID and cannot adjudicate H6.

A transfer that is later fenced or fails after service pays its transfer time and bytes before safe recomputation. No policy receives retroactive outcome knowledge.

`CONCURRENT_CANDIDATE_LOSER` represents semantic candidate concurrency, not simultaneous network-service overlap. The protocol uses one recovery action at a time because C6 closes exact single-transfer timing but does not establish a concurrent-link sharing model.

## Metric accounting

### Recovery Time

Fault/trigger time is `t=0`. First authoritative useful post-fault progress is:

```text
recompute success                recompute_seconds
transfer + successful commit     transfer_seconds
transfer + fence/failure         transfer_seconds + recompute_seconds
```

Common C1 commit duration is `ZERO_UNEVIDENCED_COMPONENT` until a separately reviewed P-SRC3/E1 measurement is introduced by an explicit future protocol version.

### State Transfer Volume

Count bytes actually transferred. A completed transfer later fenced or invalidated still counts. Recomputation transfers zero State bytes. Partial materialization is not efficiency-ranked.

### Recomputation Ratio

Each ranked Track-B point is one reusable-prefix recovery opportunity:

```text
eligible_reuse_tokens = recompute_tokens
total_input_tokens     = recompute_tokens
consumed_reuse_tokens  = recompute_tokens iff transfer authoritatively succeeds, else 0
```

This preserves the frozen C7.1 ratio definition.

### Redundant-work diagnostics

`redundant_transfer_bytes` are transferred bytes from an action that cannot become authoritative in the realized common C1 trace.

`redundant_recompute_tokens` are recompute tokens executed when a semantically authorizable, physically available transfer would have completed strictly faster under the same source-model hardware profile.

These diagnostics do not replace the frozen primary metrics.

## Hardware rule

A100 and H100 source-model strata are reported separately. A hardware-agnostic Recovery-Time support statement uses the intersection of connected support cells from both strata.

State Transfer Volume is hardware invariant, but volume-only support requires no Recovery-Time regression on either source-model stratum.

No direct physical A100/H100 claim is permitted.

A B4 support cell requires zero semantic violations and Recovery Time no worse than B3 or B0/B1/B2. It then requires either:

1. strictly lower Recovery Time than at least one relevant comparator; or
2. strictly lower State Transfer Volume than B3, with Recovery Time no worse than B3 and conservative B0/B1/B2 recompute.

A support region is a connected component of at least two adjacent cells for the same rankable scenario and support mode. Recovery-Time support must survive both hardware strata by intersection. Volume-only support is hardware invariant but must meet the no-regression rule on both profiles.

If no region exists, the efficiency classification is the predeclared null. That does not undo C4.4's correctness result.
