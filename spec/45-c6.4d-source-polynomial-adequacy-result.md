## Interpretation

Representation v3 is not admissible under its own frozen source-equivalence criterion. The H100 export satisfies the 8-ULP contract, but the A100 `attn_prefill` transcription does not. Because parent adequacy requires both hardware profiles to pass export equivalence before fresh adequacy begins, C6.4d terminates before the fresh oracle is invoked.

No inference should be drawn here about C6.4c fresh-boundary MAPE or maximum APE; those quantities were never evaluated.

The observed A100 discrepancy is consistent with a floating-point evaluation-order difference between the frozen scalar canonical arithmetic and the sklearn/NumPy source execution path. That diagnosis is structural rather than a license to alter the frozen C6.4c representation inside this observed slice. Any replacement must be independently specified and reviewed before another adequacy attempt.
