## Closed measurement contract

C4.1 fixes the evaluation boundary used by C4.2–C4.6:

```text
ground_truth
observed_evidence
policy_decision
semantic_result
```

`ground_truth` is an independent experimental oracle and is not automatically policy-visible.

O1–O4 are complete faulted-operation outcomes, not scheduler/reconciliation decision rows. Gate G1 rates are event-level metrics with explicit opportunity and violation event identities.

The causal denominator split is closed as:

```text
EXOGENOUS_PAIRED
  SAAR  stale Attempt result presentations
  WBRR  incompatible branch-reuse opportunities
  SBDR  Binding-sensitive operations
  ACR   ambiguous correctness-sensitive decisions

POLICY_DERIVED
  WSCR  actual State consumptions
  DFR   completed LogicalRequests
```

Paired exogenous opportunity events must have stable matching identities across compared policies. Policy-derived denominator events remain truthful to actual policy execution and are not fabricated to force equal coverage. Zero coverage remains `0 / 0 -> null`.

Named Gate violations remain semantically independent from O4 terminal outcomes. A recovered Gate violation may end O2, and O4 may exist without a named Gate attribution.
